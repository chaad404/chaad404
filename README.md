### 👨‍💻 About Me

I am a **Software Engineer** specializing in **AI/LLM System Architecture**, **Backend Engineering**, and **Distributed Applications**. Currently completing my BSc in Computer Science / Software Engineering at the **University of Pécs**, Hungary.

- 🔬 **Current Research & Thesis**: Engineering an automated **LLM Comparative Evaluation Framework** featuring multi-agent orchestration (LangChain, LangGraph), RAG semantic grounding, and deterministic NLP quality metrics.
- 🏗️ **Systems Engineering**: Building robust APIs, modular batch pipelines, and scalable database architectures (PostgreSQL, Spring Boot, FastAPI, Node.js).
- 🔄 **Platform Note**: Previously, the bulk of my research and development took place on **[GitLab (@rokayaarakhi)](https://gitlab.com/rokayaarakhi)**. I am currently transitioning active engineering initiatives and open-source projects here to GitHub.
- 💬 **Ask me about**: LLM Orchestration, RAG, Multi-Agent Workflows, Python, Java/Spring Boot, Docker, and Database Design.
